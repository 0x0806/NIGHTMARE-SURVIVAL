What hunts you

  • Wraith. Patrols and chases on sight. A lit torch makes you easier to spot.
  • Crawler. Fast, hears everything. Flares make it flee.
  • Stalker. Moves only while you are not looking at it. Keep it in the beam.
  • Howler. Never moves. Its scream calls everything to you.

Staying alive

  • Darkness drains your mind. Low sanity brings hallucinations and eventually hurts you.
  • Fuses glow faintly through the dark. Pills, batteries and medkits are scattered around.
